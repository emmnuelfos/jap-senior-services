Our Story Starts with Love

In Loving Memory of Ethel M. Sanders
J.A.P. Senior Care Services was founded and dedicated to our grandmother and great-grandmother, Ethel M. Sanders.
Her warmth, her dignity, and her unwavering spirit of compassion for everyone around her left a mark on our family that we carry into every home we serve.
When we built this agency, we didn't just want to start a business.
We wanted to honor her by giving other families the kind of attentive, dignified, heartfelt care that every senior deserves. Ethel's legacy is the foundation of everything we do at J.A.P.
Meet Our Founder: Yarlardo Henderson, RN
Yarlardo Henderson is a Registered Nurse with more than 25 years of experience in healthcare, spanning clinical nursing, senior living administration, and home care management.
She holds an Associate Degree in Nursing from Herzing University and has spent her entire career committed to one purpose: improving the quality of life for seniors.
Before founding J.A.P., Yarlardo served as Executive Director at Morningside of Vestavia, a senior living community where she led improvements in resident care quality, staff performance, and overall satisfaction, earning multiple industry recognitions in the process.
As owner and CRO of J.A.P. Senior Care Services, Yarlardo brings that same clinical rigor and personal warmth to home-based care.
When you choose J.A.P., you're choosing a team that is accountable to a nurse, and a nurse who is accountable to you.
Our Mission
To provide quality, compassionate home care services that meet the physical, mental, and emotional needs of every client and their family — delivered with professionalism, dignity, and genuine heart.
Our Vision
To be Jefferson and Shelby County's most trusted, innovative, and preferred provider of home and community-based care — known for excellence, accessibility, and the human connection that makes all the difference.

Meta Description
J.A.P. Senior Care Services offers companion care, personal care, private duty nursing, Alzheimer's care, 24-hour care, and more in Jefferson and Shelby County, AL.
Our Services
From a few hours of companionship to around-the-clock clinical care, J.A.P. Senior Care Services offers a full continuum of in-home care designed around your loved one's unique needs.
Every service is delivered under the oversight of our Registered Nurse founder, because quality care isn't just our promise, it's our standard.
Companion Care
Loneliness is one of the greatest challenges seniors face — and one of the most overlooked. Our companion care services are designed to provide meaningful social interaction, emotional support, and genuine human connection for seniors living at home. Whether it's sharing a meal, taking a walk, playing cards, or simply being a warm, familiar presence, our caregivers show up ready to engage.
Friendly conversation and social engagement
Accompaniment to community events and outings
Assistance with hobbies and leisure activities
Emotional support and companionship
Light meal preparation and kitchen assistance
Medication reminders
Personal Care
Maintaining personal hygiene and daily routines is essential to a senior's dignity and well-being. Our personal care services provide compassionate, respectful assistance with the activities of daily living — helping your loved one feel confident, clean, and cared for every single day.
Bathing, showering, and personal hygiene
Dressing and grooming assistance
Oral care
Mobility assistance and transfer support
Incontinence care
Morning and bedtime routines
Safety Care
For many families, peace of mind is the priority. Our safety care services are designed to reduce the risk of accidents and provide oversight for seniors who are at risk of falls, confusion, or unsafe situations at home. We work proactively to create a safer environment while preserving your loved one's independence.
Fall prevention and home safety monitoring
Supervision for seniors with mobility challenges
Medication reminders
Emergency response assistance
Safety checks and wellness monitoring
Senior Transportation
Getting to a doctor's appointment, a church service, or a family gathering should never be a barrier to a senior's quality of life. Our senior transportation services provide safe, reliable, and respectful rides so your loved one stays connected to the world around them.
Medical appointments and specialist visits
Grocery and errand runs
Social outings and community activities
Church and religious services
Family visits
Alzheimer's & Dementia Care
Caring for a loved one with Alzheimer's or dementia requires patience, specialized knowledge, and an approach centered on the whole person — not just the diagnosis. Our caregivers are trained to work with memory-impaired seniors in a way that is calm, respectful, and consistent, creating a structured environment that reduces anxiety and promotes a sense of security.
Structured daily routines and familiar environments
Redirection and de-escalation techniques
Safety supervision and wandering prevention
Engagement activities tailored to cognitive level
Family education and support resources
Coordination with healthcare providers
Respite Care
Family caregivers give so much of themselves — and even the most devoted family member needs rest, renewal, and time to recharge. Our respite care services give you the peace of mind to step away, knowing your loved one is in capable, caring hands. Whether you need a few hours or a few days, we're here.
Scheduled relief care for family caregivers
Short-term or extended coverage
All personal care and companion care services
Flexible scheduling to match your needs
Private Duty Nursing
For seniors with complex medical needs who want to remain at home, our Private Duty Nursing services provide skilled clinical care in the comfort of familiar surroundings. Our licensed RN and LPN team delivers medically advanced support that goes beyond what standard homecare aides can provide — bridging the gap between hospital-level care and the warmth of home.
Skilled nursing assessments and care plans
Wound care and post-surgical support
IV therapy and medication management
Chronic disease monitoring (diabetes, cardiac, etc.)
Coordination with physicians and specialists
Family education on care protocols
End of Life Care
When a loved one enters the final chapter of their life, what matters most is comfort, dignity, and not being alone. Our end of life care services are provided with the utmost sensitivity and compassion — supporting both the senior and the entire family through one of life's most sacred transitions. We walk this journey with you, every step of the way.
Comfort-focused personal care and hygiene
Emotional and spiritual support
Family presence and communication support
Coordination with hospice providers
24-hour availability during final stages
24-Hour Care
Some seniors require continuous care and supervision — day and night. Our 24-hour care program ensures your loved one is never alone, with a consistent team of caregivers providing uninterrupted support across all shifts. Whether recovering from a hospital stay, managing a progressive illness, or simply needing full-time presence, we're there around the clock.
Daytime and overnight caregiver coverage
Shift rotations for consistent care quality
All personal care, companion care, and safety services
Direct coordination with family and medical providers
Senior Health & Wellness
Staying healthy and engaged is one of the best ways for seniors to maintain independence and joy. Our wellness services focus on the whole person — physical, mental, and social — supporting an active, fulfilling lifestyle at home.
Light exercise and movement encouragement
Nutrition guidance and meal preparation
Hydration monitoring
Mental stimulation and cognitive activities
Community engagement and social connection
Technology Support
Technology can be a wonderful tool for seniors — helping them stay in touch with family, access telehealth appointments, and live more independently. But it can also be confusing and frustrating without the right support. Our caregivers help seniors navigate phones, tablets, video calls, and digital health devices with patience and clarity.
Smartphone and tablet setup and guidance
Video calling with family and friends
Telehealth appointment assistance
Health monitoring device support
Online safety basics

SERVICES & COVERAGE
What areas do you serve?
We serve families throughout Jefferson and Shelby County, Alabama. This includes Birmingham, Hoover, Vestavia Hills, Alabaster, Pelham, Helena, and surrounding communities.
Not sure if we cover your specific area? Call us at (205) 253-6537, and we'll let you know right away.
How do I know which services my loved one needs?
That's exactly what our free consultation is designed to help you figure out. Our team will ask you about your loved one's daily routine, health status, living situation, and the specific challenges your family is navigating.
From there, we'll recommend the services and schedule that make the most sense. You never have to figure this out alone.
GETTING STARTED
How quickly can care start?
In many cases, we can begin care within a few days of the initial consultation. If the situation is urgent, call us directly. We'll do everything we can to respond quickly.
Are your caregivers background-checked?
Yes. All J.A.P. caregivers undergo thorough background checks prior to placement. We also verify credentials and conduct ongoing supervision to ensure consistent care quality.
Is care available 24 hours a day?
Yes. We offer flexible scheduling from just a few hours per week all the way to 24-hour coverage. Our team is available around the clock, and we can accommodate urgent or after-hours care requests.
Q&A — COST & PAYMENT
Do you accept insurance or Medicaid?
We encourage families to contact us directly to discuss payment options and determine coverage eligibility. Our team can walk you through what may be available based on your loved one's situation.
WHY J.A.P.
What makes J.A.P. different from other home care agencies?
A few things stand out. First, J.A.P. is led by a Registered Nurse. This means clinical oversight is built into how we operate, not an afterthought.
Second, we are locally owned, not a franchise, so you're working with people who are part of this community, not a call center.
And third, this agency was founded out of love for our own grandmother. That personal foundation shapes everything about the way we show up.
How do I get started?
Simply call us at (205) 253-6537, send us a message at hello@japseniorservices.com, or fill out the contact form on our website.
A member of our team will be in touch to schedule a free consultation at a time that's convenient for you.
Still have questions? We'd love to hear from you. Call us any time at (205) 253-6537 or send a message to hello@japseniorservices.com.
