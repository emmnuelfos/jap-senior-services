To be built **after** Claude Design produces the mockup. Structure mirrors the Arabland presentation deck. Tone: a multi-million-dollar agency walking the client through the *why*.

### 2. The Brief
- One paragraph stating what the client asked for in their own words: a Comfort Keepers-style site, but modern, luxurious, and easy for seniors to navigate.
- 3-bullet design objectives: **Clarity** · **Premium feel** · **Accessibility-first**

### 3. Our Approach
- 4 design principles we worked from:
  1. **Editorial over clinical** — luxury wellness, not a hospital pamphlet
  2. **Big and breathable** — generous whitespace, big type, big tap targets
  3. **Photography does the heavy lifting** — warm, human, intergenerational
  4. **One CTA per moment** — never make the visitor choose between five buttons

### 4. Brand Foundation — Color Palette
- Side-by-side swatches with hex, name, and intent:
  - Deep Forest `#1F3A2E` — primary dark, trust, vitality
  - Warm Brass `#B8763E` — primary accent, used sparingly
  - Ivory `#F5F1EA` — page background (never pure white)
  - Stone Grey `#8A8782` — hairlines, secondary text
  - Charcoal `#1F1E1C` — body text
  - Soft Sage `#D7DDD2` — quote cards, subtle variation
- Short paragraph on the *why*: forest + brass is distinctive in senior care (most competitors default to clinical blues), evokes growth and warmth, contrasts beautifully against ivory.

### 5. Brand Foundation — Typography
- Display: **Fraunces** — sample at multiple sizes (h1, h2, h3, eyebrow)
- Body: **Inter at 18px** — sample paragraph
- Eyebrow: small caps, letter-spaced
- Short paragraph on the *why*: Fraunces is editorial-warm with subtle character — feels intentional. Inter at 18px is the most readable sans on the web at scale; we use it 1pt larger than industry standard because seniors are the primary readers.

### 12. Accessibility Commitments
- Body text at 18px (vs. 16px industry default)
- WCAG AA Large contrast verified
- 56px touch targets
- Click-to-call from anywhere
- No autoplay video, no auto-advancing carousels
- Keyboard-navigable, screen-reader-friendly

### 13. Animations & Interaction
- Fade-up scroll reveals (subtle, never distracting)
