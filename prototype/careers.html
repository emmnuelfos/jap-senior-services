What we look for

Character first.
Certifications second.

We can teach the rest. We hire for the way you treat someone you love.

  • Compassion. The kind that notices when something is off without being told.
  • Reliability. When you say you'll be there, you're there — five minutes early.
  • Respect for elders. Patience that's already part of who you are.
  • Strong communication. Clear with families. Calm with loved ones. Honest with us.
  • CNA or HHA certification. A plus, not a requirement — we'll help you get there.
  • A driver's license. So you can be where families need you.
  • Patience. The kind that doesn't need to be performed.
