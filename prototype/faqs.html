FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions.

We know that choosing home care for a loved one raises a lot of questions. Here are the ones we hear most often, answered honestly and clearly. If your question isn't here, please don't hesitate to call us.

Services & Coverage
What areas do you serve?

We serve families throughout Jefferson and Shelby County, Alabama. This includes Birmingham, Hoover, Vestavia Hills, Alabaster, Pelham, Helena, and surrounding communities.

Not sure if we cover your specific area? Call us at (205) 253-6537, and we'll let you know right away.

How do I know which services my loved one needs?

That's exactly what our free consultation is designed to help you figure out. Our team will ask you about your loved one's daily routine, health status, living situation, and the specific challenges your family is navigating.

From there, we'll recommend the services and schedule that make the most sense. You never have to figure this out alone.

Getting Started
How quickly can care start?

In many cases, we can begin care within a few days of the initial consultation. If the situation is urgent, call us directly. We'll do everything we can to respond quickly.

Are your caregivers background-checked?

Yes. All J.A.P. caregivers undergo thorough background checks prior to placement. We also verify credentials and conduct ongoing supervision to ensure consistent care quality.

Is care available 24 hours a day?

Yes. We offer flexible scheduling from just a few hours per week all the way to 24-hour coverage. Our team is available around the clock, and we can accommodate urgent or after-hours care requests.

Cost & Payment
Do you accept insurance or Medicaid?

We encourage families to contact us directly to discuss payment options and determine coverage eligibility. Our team can walk you through what may be available based on your loved one's situation.

Why J.A.P.
What makes J.A.P. different from other home care agencies?

A few things stand out. First, J.A.P. is led by a Registered Nurse. This means clinical oversight is built into how we operate, not an afterthought.

Second, we are locally owned, not a franchise, so you're working with people who are part of this community, not a call center.

And third, this agency was founded out of love for our own grandmother. That personal foundation shapes everything about the way we show up.
