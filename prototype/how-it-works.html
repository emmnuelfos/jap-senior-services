How it works

Care, in four
simple steps.

No long forms. No long contracts. Just a real conversation, a thoughtful plan, and care that grows with your family.

01
~ 30 min · on the phone

A free consultation.

The first step is a relaxed conversation. You'll speak with someone on our team — and if it's useful, our on-staff Registered Nurse joins too. We ask about your loved one, their day, what's working, what isn't. No script, no pressure, no commitment.

You'll leave the call with a clearer picture of what's possible. Nothing more.

02
~ 60 min · in your loved one's home

A home assessment.

If a visit feels right, we come to your loved one — not the other way around. Our RN walks the home with you, looks at mobility, safety, lighting, daily routines, and the small frictions that wear families down. We listen more than we talk.

By the end of the hour, we have what we need to write a plan. You have someone who's met your loved one as a person, not a case.

03
Built together · revised freely

A care plan tailored to your family.

Within a few days you'll have a written plan, drawn from any of our nine programs. We pick what fits — Sweet Dreams on weeknights, Up And At 'Em twice a week, a standing grocery run on Fridays — and leave space for the things only your family knows.

Nothing's locked in. You can adjust before we ever start.

04
Ongoing · no long contracts

Ongoing care, growing with you.

Care begins. Our RN checks in monthly and we revisit the plan together — adding hours when a hard week comes, scaling back when independence returns. The same caregivers, when at all possible, so your loved one builds real relationships.

If your needs ever change entirely, we'll help you find what's right — even if that means letting you go.

Frequently asked

The questions families ask first.

Do you offer one-time services?
Yes. Many of our families start with a single visit — a discharge from the hospital, a hard weekend, a recovery period after surgery — and decide from there. We're built to flex around what your family actually needs, not lock you into a schedule that doesn't fit.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. We work month-to-month. End care at any time — no penalties, no awkwardness. We'd rather earn the next month than hold you to it.
Is your nurse always involved?
Yes. Our on-staff Registered Nurse oversees every care plan, joins the home assessment when it's helpful, and checks in monthly. Day-to-day care is delivered by trained caregivers — but the plan they're following is always nurse-built.
Do you charge for travel?
A modest mileage fee applies to transportation services (medical appointments, grocery runs, family events). We're transparent about it upfront — it's never a surprise on the invoice.
What if my loved one needs more care later?
The plan grows with them. We revisit it monthly with our RN, and you can add hours, services, or programs at any time. If their needs ever exceed what in-home care can do well, we'll be honest with you and help you find the right next step.
How quickly can we start?
Most families are seen within a few days of their first call. Urgent situations — a discharge from the hospital, a sudden change at home — we work to accommodate same-week.
What areas do you serve?
We're rooted in Alabama and serve [region — to be confirmed]. If you're not sure whether your address is in our area, please call us — we'd rather hear and try than have you wonder.
How are caregivers vetted?
Every caregiver goes through background checks, reference verification, and our own conversation-based hiring process — we hire for character first, certifications second. Our RN trains every new hire on our standards before they're ever scheduled with a family.
