Program 01

Good Morning To You.

A spirited start to the day, on your loved one's pace. We help with dressing, grooming, and bed-making — gently — then a medication reminder so the morning stays on track. We finish with a quiet escort to breakfast, however they like to take it: at the table, by the window, or in their favorite chair.

What's included
  • Help with dressing and grooming
  • Bed-making and light tidy
  • Medication reminder
  • Escort to breakfast

Program 02

Welcome Home.

Moving — even just down the road — is rarely simple at any age. Welcome Home is a soft landing. We help unpack thoughtfully, set up the spaces your loved one will use most, and add the small touches that make a new place feel like theirs. A light cleaning, a familiar hand, and a few hours of quiet help.

What's included
  • Unpacking and thoughtful setup
  • Light cleaning of priority spaces
  • Familiar comforts laid out — photographs, blankets, books
  • Orientation to the new home and neighbors

Program 04

Sweet Dreams.

The end of the day matters as much as the start. Sweet Dreams is a quiet evening rhythm: a light snack, into nightwear, teeth brushed, medication taken, and the bed turned down. Lamp lighting low, water glass on the table, the kind of routine that turns "going to sleep" back into "winding down."

What's included
  • A light evening snack
  • Help into nightwear
  • Teeth brushing and bedtime grooming
  • Medication reminder
  • Bed turndown and a final tidy

Program 05

Discharge Assistance.

Leaving the hospital is rarely the end of a hard day. Discharge Assistance is the bridge from hospital to home. We coordinate the transportation, sit through the discharge instructions with your family, fill the prescriptions, and walk your loved one back through their own front door — so the first night home is calm, not chaotic.

What's included
  • Transportation home from the hospital or facility
  • Help understanding discharge instructions
  • Prescription pick-up and organization
  • A safety walk-through of the home
  • Hand-off to ongoing care, if needed
