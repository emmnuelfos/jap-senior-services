About Us

Family-owned. Family-run.

J.A.P. Senior Care Services started 25 years ago, in honor of our grandmother Ethel M. Sanders. The care she received changed everything. We've been giving that same care to Alabama families ever since.

We're privately held and locally rooted. We show up early, listen longer, and treat your loved one the way we'd treat our own.

No franchise model. No corporate scripts. Just care, from one family to another.
