Our story

Started by family.
Built for yours.

J.A.P Senior Services was founded by a family who watched their grandmother, Ethel M. Sanders, receive the kind of attentive, dignified care that changes everything. Twenty-five years later, that same spirit guides every visit we make.

We're privately held, locally rooted, and quietly committed to the families we serve — the kind of provider who shows up early, listens longer, and treats your loved one the way we'd treat our own.

No franchise model. No corporate scripts. Just care, from one family to another.

How it works

Getting started is simple.

No long forms. No long contracts. Just a real conversation and a plan that fits your family.

01 — A free consultation.
We listen to what your family needs.

A thirty-minute call with our team — or our on-staff RN if that's helpful. No pressure, no obligation, no script.

02 — A care plan made for them.
Built around their daily life.

We design a plan with our nurse, drawing from any of our nine programs — and tuned to your loved one's routine, not ours.

03 — Care that grows with you.
Adjust as needs change.

No long contracts. We check in monthly, and the plan grows or scales back as your family does.
