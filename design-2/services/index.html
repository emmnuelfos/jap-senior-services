Our services

A full continuum of in-home care

From a few hours of companionship to around-the-clock clinical care, every service is delivered under the oversight of our Registered Nurse founder — because quality care isn’t just our promise, it’s our standard.

01

Companion Care

Loneliness is one of the greatest challenges seniors face — and one of the most overlooked. Our companion care services provide meaningful social interaction, emotional support, and genuine human connection for seniors living at home. Whether it’s sharing a meal, taking a walk, playing cards, or simply being a warm, familiar presence, our caregivers show up ready to engage.

  • Friendly conversation and social engagement
  • Accompaniment to community events and outings
  • Assistance with hobbies and leisure activities
  • Emotional support and companionship
  • Light meal preparation and kitchen assistance
  • Medication reminders
Request This Service
02

Personal Care

Maintaining personal hygiene and daily routines is essential to a senior’s dignity and well-being. Our personal care services provide compassionate, respectful assistance with the activities of daily living — helping your loved one feel confident, clean, and cared for every single day.

  • Bathing, showering, and personal hygiene
  • Dressing and grooming assistance
  • Oral care
  • Mobility assistance and transfer support
  • Incontinence care
  • Morning and bedtime routines
Request This Service
03

Safety Care

For many families, peace of mind is the priority. Our safety care services are designed to reduce the risk of accidents and provide oversight for seniors who are at risk of falls, confusion, or unsafe situations at home. We work proactively to create a safer environment while preserving your loved one’s independence.

  • Fall prevention and home safety monitoring
  • Supervision for seniors with mobility challenges
  • Medication reminders
  • Emergency response assistance
  • Safety checks and wellness monitoring
Request This Service
04

Senior Transportation

Getting to a doctor’s appointment, a church service, or a family gathering should never be a barrier to a senior’s quality of life. Our senior transportation services provide safe, reliable, and respectful rides so your loved one stays connected to the world around them.

  • Medical appointments and specialist visits
  • Grocery and errand runs
  • Social outings and community activities
  • Church and religious services
  • Family visits
Request This Service
05

Alzheimer’s & Dementia Care

Caring for a loved one with Alzheimer’s or dementia requires patience, specialized knowledge, and an approach centered on the whole person — not just the diagnosis. Our caregivers are trained to work with memory-impaired seniors in a way that is calm, respectful, and consistent, creating a structured environment that reduces anxiety and promotes a sense of security.

  • Structured daily routines and familiar environments
  • Redirection and de-escalation techniques
  • Safety supervision and wandering prevention
  • Engagement activities tailored to cognitive level
  • Family education and support resources
  • Coordination with healthcare providers
Request This Service
06

Respite Care

Family caregivers give so much of themselves — and even the most devoted family member needs rest, renewal, and time to recharge. Our respite care services give you the peace of mind to step away, knowing your loved one is in capable, caring hands. Whether you need a few hours or a few days, we’re here.

  • Scheduled relief care for family caregivers
  • Short-term or extended coverage
  • All personal care and companion care services
  • Flexible scheduling to match your needs
Request This Service
07

Private Duty Nursing

For seniors with complex medical needs who want to remain at home, our Private Duty Nursing services provide skilled clinical care in the comfort of familiar surroundings. Our licensed RN and LPN team delivers medically advanced support that goes beyond what standard homecare aides can provide — bridging the gap between hospital-level care and the warmth of home.

  • Skilled nursing assessments and care plans
  • Wound care and post-surgical support
  • IV therapy and medication management
  • Chronic disease monitoring (diabetes, cardiac, etc.)
  • Coordination with physicians and specialists
  • Family education on care protocols
Request This Service
08

End of Life Care

When a loved one enters the final chapter of their life, what matters most is comfort, dignity, and not being alone. Our end of life care services are provided with the utmost sensitivity and compassion — supporting both the senior and the entire family through one of life’s most sacred transitions. We walk this journey with you, every step of the way.

  • Comfort-focused personal care and hygiene
  • Emotional and spiritual support
  • Family presence and communication support
  • Coordination with hospice providers
  • 24-hour availability during final stages
Request This Service
09

24-Hour Care

Some seniors require continuous care and supervision — day and night. Our 24-hour care program ensures your loved one is never alone, with a consistent team of caregivers providing uninterrupted support across all shifts. Whether recovering from a hospital stay, managing a progressive illness, or simply needing full-time presence, we’re there around the clock.

  • Daytime and overnight caregiver coverage
  • Shift rotations for consistent care quality
  • All personal care, companion care, and safety services
  • Direct coordination with family and medical providers

10

Senior Health & Wellness

Staying healthy and engaged is one of the best ways for seniors to maintain independence and joy. Our wellness services focus on the whole person — physical, mental, and social — supporting an active, fulfilling lifestyle at home.

  • Light exercise and movement encouragement
  • Nutrition guidance and meal preparation
  • Hydration monitoring
  • Mental stimulation and cognitive activities
  • Community engagement and social connection
Request This Service
11

Technology Support

Technology can be a wonderful tool for seniors — helping them stay in touch with family, access telehealth appointments, and live more independently. But it can also be confusing and frustrating without the right support. Our caregivers help seniors navigate phones, tablets, video calls, and digital health devices with patience and clarity.

  • Smartphone and tablet setup and guidance
  • Video calling with family and friends
  • Telehealth appointment assistance
  • Health monitoring device support
  • Online safety basics
