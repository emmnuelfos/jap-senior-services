What we look for

Requirements

  • A genuine passion for caring for older adults
  • Reliability, punctuality, and professionalism
  • Strong communication skills with clients, families, and team members
  • Background check clearance
  • Valid Alabama driver’s license (required for transportation roles)
  • CNA or HHA certification (for applicable roles)
